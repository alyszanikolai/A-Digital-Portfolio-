## 📋 Activity Description
This activity involved conceptualizing and designing a personal brand identity, including a personal logo, color palette, typography choices, and visual style tailored to represent myself as a 4th-year nursing student.

## 💡 What Have I Learned?
* **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how consistent colors, fonts, and visual elements work together to communicate who you are and create a recognizable professional identity.
* **Challenge & Solution:** Building a personal brand was challenging because I wasn't completely sure what elements to include or how to represent myself visually. I solved this by exploring different branding examples and gathering ideas until I figured out a clean, cohesive style that felt like me.
* **Future Application:** I can apply this personal branding concept to future professional portfolios, digital resumes, and career profiles as I transition from nursing school into clinical practice.
