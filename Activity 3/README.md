## 📋 Activity Description
This activity required creating an informative social media infographic paired with project documentation to communicate health and digital concepts clearly to an online audience.

## 💡 What Have I Learned?
* **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how to organize key information visually using clear headings, icons, and readable layouts suited for social media viewing.
* **Challenge & Solution:** While the actual process of putting the infographic together went smoothly, figuring out what topic and visual direction to make was the tricky part. I overcame this by browsing design samples on Canva to spark ideas on how to structure and style the information effectively.
* **Future Application:** In community health nursing and patient education, I can use these infographic techniques to create easy-to-read health awareness posts and wellness guides.
