## 📋 Activity Description
This activity focused on designing and building a functional website concept for a clothing company, applying web layout structure, visual merchandising principles, and media organization.

## 💡 What Have I Learned?
* **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I gained hands-on experience in structuring an e-commerce style webpage, organizing product sections, and aligning visual assets to match a clothing brand's aesthetic.
* **Challenge & Solution:** Finding and fixing specific product photos that matched the exact clothing items I envisioned for the brand was difficult. To keep the website layout clean and functional without stalling progress, I settled on well-matched temporary placeholder pictures that accurately represented the apparel pieces.
* **Future Application:** In future web projects, I can replace these temporary slots with original product photography and expand the site with interactive size guides or lookbook galleries.
