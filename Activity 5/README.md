## 📋 Activity Description
This activity explored the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools for generating text and visual assets, focusing on how prompt precision directly affects the quality and realism of AI-generated outputs.

## 💡 What Have I Learned?
* **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned that AI tools require highly specific, structured commands to produce useful outputs rather than vague, one-line instructions.
* **Challenge & Solution:** Two major challenges were figuring out how to phrase exact commands to get the output I envisioned, and dealing with AI-generated pictures that turned out distorted or creepy-looking. I addressed this by refining my prompts with clearer style descriptions and constraints, and carefully reviewing every generated result instead of accepting the first output.
* **Future Application:** Moving forward, I will combine more descriptive prompt writing with careful human judgment—using AI only as a starting point for drafts and ideas rather than relying blindly on raw generated visuals.
