## 📋 Activity Description
This activity explored resume generation tools and layout formatting to create a realistic, professional curriculum vitae showcasing my true-to-life nursing background, clinical skills, and academic qualifications.

## 💡 What Have I Learned?
* **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how to organize realistic clinical experiences, trainings, and core competencies into a clean, easy-to-scan resume format.
* **Challenge & Solution:** While AI resume generators are fast, they tend to produce uniform, robotic phrasing that looks obviously AI-written—which could hurt your chances of getting hired. To solve this, I wrote the content myself using my true-to-life details and personal insights so the resume stayed authentic while keeping a polished layout.
* **Future Application:** When applying for clinical nursing roles and hospital positions after graduation, I will continue building and customizing my resume personally so employers see my genuine voice and real qualifications rather than a generic template.
