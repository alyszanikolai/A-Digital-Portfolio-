## 📋 Activity Description
A collaborative group PowerPoint presentation exploring how Google Forms and Google Meet work together to gather responses, streamline online communication, and keep virtual discussions connected.

## 💡 What Have I Learned?
* **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how to present technical features of Google Forms and Google Meet in a structured way that highlights practical everyday collaboration.
* **Challenge & Solution:** Since our topic covered familiar productivity tools, the hardest part was figuring out how to make the PowerPoint and delivery enticing enough so the class would actually listen and participate. We solved this by designing more interactive, visually engaging slides and planning a creative delivery flow rather than just reading off definitions.
* **Future Application:** Knowing how to make informational topics engaging will help me lead interactive health teachings, patient seminars, and team briefings where active participation matters.
